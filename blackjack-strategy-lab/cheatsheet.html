Blackjack Cheat Sheet

6 decks · dealer stands soft 17 · double after split · late surrender · 3:2. Basic strategy + Hi-Lo counting.

Before you sit down (biggest edge, zero skill)

  • Blackjack pays 3:2, never 6:5
  • Dealer stands on soft 17
  • Double after split allowed
  • Late surrender offered
  • Deep penetration, fewer decks

4. Count-based plays that matter

  • Take insurance only at true count +3 or higher.
  • 16 vs 10: stand when true count is 0 or positive (else follow the grid).

5. Worked example

Shuffle: running count 0. You tally tags all shoe. It reaches +9.
~3 decks left → 9 ÷ 3 = TC +3 → bet 4 units.
Later, ~1.5 decks left, still +9 → 9 ÷ 1.5 = TC +6 → bet 12 units.

Bankroll reality

Measured edge ≈ +1 unit / 100 hands. You need roughly 1,000+ units to keep risk of ruin near 5%. Expect long losing streaks even when you are ahead long-run.

Basic strategy only minimizes the house edge (~0.3–0.5%); it does not win. Hi-Lo counting is the one legal way to turn a small edge in your favor, and it is a high-variance grind. Casinos bar counters. This is the mathematics of advantage play, not financial advice.
